## 1. Overview

Special Service Requests (SSRs) are standardized IATA codes used to communicate specific passenger needs throughout the travel journey. This document provides a complete reference for all SSR codes accepted by the airline, including definitions, eligibility criteria, request deadlines, and handling procedures.

SSRs are added to the passenger's PNR and transmitted to all relevant parties (ground handling, catering, cabin crew, destination station).

## 2. Wheelchair & Mobility Assistance

### 2.1 Wheelchair SSR Codes

| SSR Code | Full Name | Description | Details |
|----------|-----------|-------------|---------|
| **WCHR** | Wheelchair – Ramp | Passenger can walk short distances but needs wheelchair for long distances (to/from aircraft, between terminals). | Can ascend/descend aircraft steps. Can walk to/from seat. |
| **WCHC** | Wheelchair – Cabin Seat | Passenger is completely immobile. Requires wheelchair to/from aircraft and must be carried to/from cabin seat. | Cannot walk at all. Requires lifting assistance. Cannot ascend/descend steps. |
| **WCHS** | Wheelchair – Steps | Passenger can walk to/from cabin seat but cannot ascend/descend aircraft steps. Requires wheelchair on the ramp and assistance on steps. | Can walk short distances on flat surfaces only. |
| **WCBD** | Wheelchair – Dry Cell Battery | Passenger travels with their own battery-powered wheelchair. Wheelchair is stowed in the aircraft hold. | Battery type must be specified (dry cell, wet cell, lithium). |
| **WCBW** | Wheelchair – Wet Cell Battery | Passenger travels with a wet cell battery-powered wheelchair. | Battery must be disconnected and terminals insulated. Wheelchair stowed upright. |
| **WCMP** | Wheelchair – Manual Power | Passenger travels with a manual (non-powered) wheelchair. | Foldable wheelchairs stowed in cabin if space permits; otherwise in hold. |

### 2.2 Wheelchair Request Deadlines

| SSR Code | Online / App | Contact Centre | Airport |
|----------|-------------|----------------|---------|
| WCHR | At booking or up to **48 hours** before departure | Up to **48 hours** before departure | Up to **24 hours** before departure |
| WCHC | At booking or up to **48 hours** before departure | Up to **48 hours** before departure | **Not available** (must be pre-booked) |
| WCHS | At booking or up to **48 hours** before departure | Up to **48 hours** before departure | Up to **24 hours** before departure |
| WCBD/WCBW | At booking or up to **72 hours** before departure | Up to **72 hours** before departure | **Not available** (must be pre-booked) |
| WCMP | At booking or up to **48 hours** before departure | Up to **48 hours** before departure | Up to **24 hours** before departure |

### 2.3 Wheelchair Handling Notes

- There is **no charge** for wheelchair assistance services.
- Airlines are required to accept wheelchairs as checked baggage at **no additional cost** (including powered wheelchairs up to 1 unit per passenger).
- The airline provides airport wheelchairs free of charge between check-in and the aircraft, and between the aircraft and baggage claim at the destination.
- Damaged or lost wheelchairs must be reported immediately. The airline is liable for repair or replacement and must provide a temporary wheelchair within **24 hours**.
- Maximum **2 WCHC passengers** per narrow-body aircraft and **4 WCHC passengers** per wide-body aircraft (safety regulation).

### 3.2 Special Meal Handling Notes

- Special meals are available **at no additional charge** on all flights where meal service is provided.
- Only **one special meal code** can be applied per passenger per flight segment.
- If a special meal is not available or was not loaded, offer the passenger the closest alternative from the standard meal options and log an incident report.
- Confirm special meal requests with the passenger at check-in.
- Cabin crew should deliver special meals **before** the standard meal service begins.

## 4. Unaccompanied Minor (UMNR)

### 4.1 UMNR SSR Code

| SSR Code | Full Name | Age Range | Service Type |
|----------|-----------|-----------|-------------|
| **UMNR** | Unaccompanied Minor | **5–11 years** | Mandatory escort service |

### 4.2 UMNR Rules & Requirements

| Requirement | Detail |
|-------------|--------|
| **Age range** | 5–11 years (children under 5 must travel with an adult). Children 12–15 may optionally use the UM service. |
| **Booking deadline** | **48 hours** before departure (must be booked via Contact Centre or airport; not available online). |
| **Route restriction** | Direct flights only. No connecting flights for mandatory UMNR (ages 5–11). Optional UMNR (12–15) may travel on connections with a minimum 2-hour connection time. |
| **Flight restriction** | Not available on the last flight of the day, red-eye flights, or flights with planned technical stops. |
| **Documentation** | UMNR handling form (3 copies: airline, departing guardian, receiving guardian). Valid passport or ID. Signed consent form from parent/legal guardian. |
| **Guardian requirement** | Departing guardian must remain at the airport until the flight has departed. Receiving guardian must be present at the arrival airport with valid photo ID matching the name on the UMNR form. |
| **Max UMNR per flight** | Maximum **4 UMNRs** per flight (narrow-body) or **8 UMNRs** per flight (wide-body). |
| **Seating** | UMNR passengers are seated in a designated area near the cabin crew station. Not in exit rows. |
| **Fee** | EUR 50–EUR 75 per direction (see Ancillary Services Catalogue KB-ANC-001). |

### 4.3 UMNR Handling Procedure

1. Departing guardian completes and signs the UMNR handling form at the check-in desk.
2. Airline staff escort the child through security and to the boarding gate.
3. Child boards the aircraft first, before general boarding.
4. Cabin crew are briefed and the child is supervised throughout the flight.
5. On arrival, the child disembarks last, escorted by cabin crew.
6. Airline ground staff deliver the child to the receiving guardian at the arrival hall.
7. Receiving guardian must present valid photo ID and sign the UMNR form to take custody.
8. If the receiving guardian does not arrive, contact the emergency phone number on the UMNR form. If unreachable after 2 hours, contact local child protection services.

## 5. Pet Transport

### 5.1 Pet SSR Codes

| SSR Code | Full Name | Description | Request Deadline |
|----------|-----------|-------------|-----------------|
| **PETC** | Pet in Cabin | Small pet traveling in the passenger cabin in an approved carrier under the seat. | **48 hours** before departure |
| **AVIH** | Animal in Hold | Pet traveling in a climate-controlled section of the aircraft hold in an IATA-approved crate. | **48 hours** before departure |

### 5.2 Pet Transport Rules

| Rule | PETC (Cabin) | AVIH (Hold) |
|------|-------------|-------------|
| **Max weight** | 8 kg (pet + carrier) | 75 kg (pet + crate) |
| **Carrier/crate size** | Max 55 x 40 x 23 cm (soft-sided) | IATA-approved rigid crate, sized for animal to stand, turn, lie down |
| **Pets per passenger** | 1 | 2 (in separate crates) |
| **Pets per flight** | 2 in cabin | 3 in hold |
| **Species allowed** | Dogs, cats | Dogs, cats, rabbits, ferrets |
| **Health documentation** | Veterinary health certificate (<10 days old), vaccination records (rabies), EU Pet Passport (for EU travel) | Same as PETC + import permit if applicable |
| **Breed restrictions** | Brachycephalic (flat-nosed) breeds allowed in cabin only | Brachycephalic breeds NOT accepted in hold (October–April exceptions for temp <25°C) |
| **Temperature restrictions** | N/A | Not accepted when ground temp >30°C or <-10°C at origin or destination |

### 5.3 Service / Assistance Animals

- Service animals (guide dogs, hearing dogs, psychiatric service dogs) travel **free of charge** in the cabin.
- SSR code: **SVAN** (Service Animal)
- Documentation: Valid service animal certification, vaccination records, health certificate.
- Request deadline: **24 hours** before departure.
- Service animals are not subject to the cabin pet limit.
- Emotional support animals are **not** accepted as service animals and must follow standard PETC/AVIH rules.

### 6.1 Medical Assistance Codes

| SSR Code | Full Name | Description | Request Deadline |
|----------|-----------|-------------|-----------------|
| **MEDA** | Medical Case | Passenger with a medical condition that requires special attention or clearance to fly. Requires MEDIF form completion. | **72 hours** before departure |
| **OXYG** | Oxygen | Passenger requires supplemental oxygen during the flight. Only approved portable oxygen concentrators (POC) are permitted. | **72 hours** before departure |

- All MEDA, OXYG, and STCR requests require medical clearance from the airline's Medical Department.
- See Medical Clearance Guide (KB-MED-001) for detailed MEDIF form requirements and clearance procedures.
- Medical clearance processing time: **48–72 hours**.

## 7. Sensory Impairment Codes

| SSR Code | Full Name | Description | Request Deadline |
|----------|-----------|-------------|-----------------|
| **DEAF** | Hearing Impaired | Passenger with significant hearing loss or deafness. Requires visual safety briefing and visual notifications for announcements. | **24 hours** before departure |
| **BLND** | Blind / Visually Impaired | Passenger who is blind or has significant visual impairment. Requires verbal safety briefing and physical guidance through the airport and aircraft. | **24 hours** before departure |

### 7.1 Handling Notes — DEAF

- Provide written safety briefing card and ensure the passenger has read it.
- Use written notes or text messages for communication (gate changes, delays, etc.).
- Seat near cabin crew for easy visual communication.
- Alert cabin crew to tap the passenger's shoulder for important announcements.
- Ensure visual emergency signals are working in the passenger's area.

### 7.2 Handling Notes — BLND

- Offer pre-boarding to familiarize with the seat and surroundings.
- Provide verbal safety briefing with tactile demonstration of seatbelt, life vest, and oxygen mask.
- Describe meal contents verbally before serving.
- Escort to/from lavatory upon request.
- Guide dogs travel free in the cabin (see SVAN above).
- At arrival, escort to baggage claim and ground transportation.

## 9. SSR Processing Rules

### 9.1 General Rules

1. All SSRs must be entered in the PNR **before ticketing** when possible.
2. SSRs added after ticketing must be confirmed by the operating carrier.
3. Multiple SSR codes can be applied to a single passenger (e.g., WCHR + DBML + DEAF).
4. SSR requests are subject to **availability** and operational feasibility.
5. The airline will make **reasonable efforts** to accommodate all SSR requests but cannot guarantee all services on all flights.

### 9.2 SSR Status Codes

| Status | Meaning |
|--------|---------|
| **NN** | Requested (awaiting confirmation) |
| **HK** | Confirmed |
| **UC** | Unable to confirm (waitlisted) |
| **UN** | Unable (rejected) |
| **XX** | Cancelled |

### 9.3 Confirmation Workflow

1. Agent enters SSR in PNR with status NN.
2. SSR is transmitted to the operating carrier and ground handlers.
3. Operating carrier responds with HK (confirmed) or UC/UN (unable).
4. Agent informs the passenger of the SSR status.
5. If an SSR is rejected (UN), the agent must:
   - Explain the reason to the passenger.
   - Offer alternatives where possible.
   - Escalate to the Special Services desk (extension 2800) if needed.
