## 1. Overview

This catalogue details all purchasable ancillary services available to passengers. Services can be purchased at various stages: during booking, post-booking via Manage My Booking, at the airport check-in desk, or at the boarding gate (limited services). Prices listed are per passenger, per segment unless otherwise stated.

## 4. Seat Selection

### 4.1 Seat Selection Pricing

| Seat Category | Price Range | Description |
|--------------|-------------|-------------|
| **Standard seat** | Free (Classic, Flex, Business) | Standard Economy or Business seat |
| **Standard seat** | EUR 5–EUR 10 (Light fare) | Standard Economy seat for Light fare holders |
| **Front row / Bulkhead** | EUR 15–EUR 35 | Extra space, first off the aircraft |
| **Extra legroom** | EUR 29–EUR 69 | Exit row or premium Economy seats (≥34" pitch) |
| **Preferred seat** | EUR 10–EUR 20 | Window/aisle in forward Economy rows |
| **Duo seat (empty middle)** | EUR 25–EUR 45 | Guarantees the middle seat next to you remains empty |
| **Business window/aisle** | Free | Included with Business fare |

### 4.2 Seat Selection Rules

- **Light fare:** No seat selection included. Can purchase at booking or later. If no seat is selected, one is assigned at check-in.
- **Classic fare:** Free standard seat selection at booking. Extra legroom and front row seats available for a fee.
- **Flex fare:** Free seat selection including preferred seats. Extra legroom available for a fee.
- **Business fare:** Free seat selection of any Business class seat.
- **Families:** Passengers traveling with children under 12 will be seated together at no extra charge, even if specific seats were not selected. This is a regulatory requirement.
- **Seat changes:** Purchased seat selections are non-refundable but can be changed to a seat of equal or higher value (paying the difference).

### 4.3 Exit Row Restrictions

Passengers seated in exit rows must:
- Be at least **16 years old**
- Be physically capable of operating the emergency exit (no mobility restrictions)
- Understand safety instructions in English or the local language
- Not be traveling with infants or small children
- Not require a seatbelt extension
