## 1. Overview

This document defines the rules, fees, and procedures governing voluntary booking changes for all fare families operated by the airline. All agents must adhere to these policies when processing passenger-initiated changes. Changes initiated due to airline irregular operations (IROP) are governed separately by the Disruption Handling Guide (KB-DIS-001).

## 3. Same-Day Change Rules

### 3.1 Same-Day Confirmed Change

- **Eligibility:** Available to Classic, Flex, Business Saver, and Business Flex fare holders.
- **Fee:** EUR 25 flat fee (all channels), regardless of route distance.
- **Conditions:**
  - Must be requested on the **day of departure** (calendar day based on departure city local time).
  - Only available for flights on the **same route** (origin and destination must match).
  - Subject to **seat availability** in the same or lower cabin class.
  - Fare difference applies if the new flight has a higher fare. No refund if the new flight fare is lower.
  - Cannot be combined with other promotional offers or discount codes.

### 3.2 Same-Day Standby

- **Eligibility:** Available to Flex, Business Saver, and Business Flex fare holders only.
- **Fee:** Free of charge.
- **Conditions:**
  - Passenger must be present at the departure gate at least **45 minutes before the earlier flight's departure**.
  - Boarding is subject to **available seats** after all confirmed passengers have boarded.
  - If standby is unsuccessful, the original booking remains valid.
  - Standby is only available for **earlier flights** on the same day and same route.
  - Passenger must check in for both the original and standby flights.
  - Baggage will be transferred automatically if standby is confirmed at least 30 minutes before departure.

## 4. Deadline Rules

### 4.1 Minimum Change Deadline

- All voluntary changes must be requested **at least 3 hours before the scheduled departure time** of the original flight.
- Changes requested less than 3 hours before departure will be **declined**. The passenger must either:
  - Travel on the original flight, or
  - Treat the ticket as a no-show (subject to no-show policy for their fare family).

### 4.2 No-Show Consequences by Fare Family

| Fare Family       | No-Show Consequence |
|--------------------|---------------------|
| **Light**          | Ticket forfeited entirely. No refund. No rebooking. |
| **Classic**        | Return segment cancelled. Taxes may be refundable upon request. |
| **Flex**           | Ticket retains value minus EUR 50 no-show fee. Rebookable within 12 months. |
| **Business Saver** | Ticket retains value minus EUR 50 no-show fee. Rebookable within 12 months. |
| **Business Flex**  | Ticket retains full value. Rebookable within 12 months. No penalty. |

### 4.3 Post-Departure Changes

- Changes to the return segment of a booked itinerary may be made **after the outbound segment has been flown**, subject to fare family rules and applicable fees.
- The outbound segment cannot be changed after departure.

## 5. Route Change Rules

### 5.1 Permitted Route Changes

- Route changes (change of origin and/or destination) are treated as a **cancellation and rebooking**.
- The original ticket is cancelled, and applicable cancellation fees apply per fare family.
- A new ticket is issued at the **current available fare** on the new route.
- No fare credit is carried over from the original ticket to the new route unless fare rules explicitly allow it.

### 5.2 Exceptions

- **Within Same City Pair:** Changes between airports serving the same city (e.g., London Heathrow to London Gatwick) are treated as a standard change, not a route change. Standard change fees apply.
- **Codeshare Flights:** Route changes involving codeshare partners require revalidation. The agent must verify that the new route is available under the same codeshare agreement.
- **Connecting Flights:** If the route change affects a connecting itinerary, the minimum connection time (MCT) must be validated for the new routing. Standard MCT is:
  - Domestic to Domestic: 45 minutes
  - Domestic to International: 90 minutes
  - International to International: 60 minutes

### 5.3 Route Change Process

1. Calculate cancellation penalty on the original booking.
2. Search for availability on the new requested route and date.
3. Quote the new fare to the passenger, clearly stating:
   - Cancellation fee from original booking
   - New fare amount
   - Any fare difference (positive or negative)
4. Obtain passenger confirmation before processing.
5. Cancel original booking segments.
6. Issue new ticket on the requested route.
7. Send updated confirmation to passenger.

## 6. Cabin Upgrade Rules

### 6.1 Voluntary Cabin Upgrade at Time of Change

When processing a booking change, passengers may request a cabin upgrade. The following rules apply:

| Original Cabin | Upgrade To        | Upgrade Fee        | Fare Difference |
|----------------|--------------------|--------------------|-----------------|
| Economy Light  | Economy Classic    | Not permitted (Light is non-changeable) | N/A |
| Economy Classic| Economy Flex       | EUR 50 + fare diff | Yes             |
| Economy Classic| Business Saver    | EUR 50 + fare diff | Yes             |
| Economy Classic| Business Flex     | EUR 50 + fare diff | Yes             |
| Economy Flex   | Business Saver    | Free + fare diff   | Yes             |
| Economy Flex   | Business Flex     | Free + fare diff   | Yes             |
| Business Saver | Business Flex     | Free + fare diff   | Yes             |

> **Note:** The EUR 50 upgrade processing fee for Classic fares is **in addition to** the standard change fee and fare difference.

### 6.2 Cabin Downgrade

- Voluntary cabin downgrades are treated as a cancellation and rebooking.
- The passenger will receive a refund of the fare difference **only if their fare family permits refunds**.
- Light and Classic fare passengers will not receive a fare difference refund for voluntary downgrades.

### 6.3 Operational Cabin Upgrades (Complimentary)

- When a flight is oversold in Economy, the airline may offer complimentary upgrades to Business class.
- Priority for complimentary upgrades:
  1. Loyalty program Platinum and Gold members
  2. Passengers on Flex fares
  3. Passengers on Classic fares (by check-in order)
- Complimentary upgrades are noted in the PNR with remark code `OPUP` and do not affect the fare basis of the ticket.

### 6.4 Bid Upgrade Program

- Passengers on eligible routes may submit upgrade bids via the airline's website or app.
- Bids are accepted **between 72 hours and 24 hours before departure**.
- Minimum and maximum bid amounts vary by route and are displayed at the time of bidding.
- Successful bids are confirmed via email **6 hours before departure**.
- Bid upgrades are non-refundable once confirmed.

## 7. Multiple Change Rules

- There is **no limit** to the number of times a Flex or Business fare ticket can be changed, provided each change is made within the deadline.
- Classic fare tickets may be changed a **maximum of 3 times**. Each change incurs the applicable fee.
- After the 3rd change on a Classic fare, the ticket must be cancelled and rebooked.

---

## 8. Group Booking Changes

- Group bookings (10+ passengers) follow separate change rules managed by the Group Desk.
- Change deadline for groups: **7 days before departure** (not 3 hours).
- Name changes within a group are permitted up to **48 hours before departure** at no fee for up to 10% of the group size.
- Contact the Group Desk at groups@airline.com or extension 4500.

## 10. Agent Processing Notes

1. Always verify the fare family before quoting a change fee. Fare family is displayed in the PNR under the fare basis code.
2. Collect the change fee **before** processing the change in the reservation system.
3. Issue an EMD (Electronic Miscellaneous Document) for all change fees collected.
4. If a fare difference applies, collect the fare difference and reissue the ticket.
5. Send a change confirmation email to the passenger immediately after processing.
6. Log all changes in the PNR history with a remark noting: agent ID, date/time, change type, and fee collected.
7. For disputed change fees, escalate to the Fare Audit team (extension 3200).
