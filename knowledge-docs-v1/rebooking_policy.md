## 1. Overview

This document outlines the rebooking policies for both voluntary (passenger-initiated) and involuntary (airline-initiated) rebookings. All customer service agents must follow these guidelines when rebooking passengers across all channels (contact centre, airport, digital).

---

## 2. Voluntary Rebooking

### 2.1 Voluntary Rebooking Fees by Fare Family

Voluntary rebooking occurs when a passenger requests to move their journey to a different flight or date for personal reasons.

| Fare Family       | Short-Haul (<1,500 km) | Medium-Haul (1,500–3,500 km) | Long-Haul (>3,500 km) |
|--------------------|------------------------|-------------------------------|------------------------|
| **Light**          | Not rebookable          | Not rebookable                | Not rebookable         |
| **Classic**        | EUR 75                  | EUR 100                       | EUR 150                |
| **Flex**           | Free                    | Free                          | Free                   |
| **Business Saver** | Free                    | Free                          | Free                   |
| **Business Flex**  | Free                    | Free                          | Free                   |

> **Note:** Light fare passengers who wish to travel on a different flight must cancel (forfeiting the ticket) and purchase a new ticket.

### 2.2 Fare Difference Handling — Voluntary Rebooking

When a passenger voluntarily rebooks to a different flight, the fare difference between the original and new flight is handled as follows:

| Scenario | Action |
|----------|--------|
| **New fare is higher** | Passenger must pay the fare difference in addition to any applicable rebooking fee. |
| **New fare is equal** | Only the rebooking fee (if applicable) is charged. |
| **New fare is lower** | No refund of fare difference for Classic fares. Flex and Business fares receive the difference as a travel voucher valid for 12 months. |

**Fare difference calculation:**
- Compare the **fare basis** of the original ticket with the **current available fare** on the new flight in the same fare family.
- Taxes and surcharges are recalculated based on the new routing and departure date.
- If the total new fare (including taxes) exceeds the original, the passenger pays the difference.

### 2.3 Voluntary Rebooking Process

1. Verify the passenger's identity and booking reference (PNR).
2. Confirm the fare family and check rebooking eligibility.
3. Search for available flights on the requested date/route.
4. Calculate the rebooking fee and any fare difference.
5. Clearly communicate the total cost to the passenger:
   - Rebooking fee: EUR [amount]
   - Fare difference: EUR [amount] (if applicable)
   - Total charge: EUR [amount]
6. Obtain verbal or written confirmation from the passenger.
7. Process the rebooking in the reservation system.
8. Collect payment (rebooking fee + fare difference).
9. Issue an EMD for the rebooking fee.
10. Reissue the ticket with the new flight details.
11. Send a rebooking confirmation email to the passenger.

## 3. Involuntary Rebooking

### 3.1 Definition

Involuntary rebooking occurs when the airline initiates a change due to:
- Flight cancellation
- Significant schedule change (>2 hours)
- Aircraft downgrade or equipment change
- Overbooking / denied boarding
- Weather or ATC (Air Traffic Control) disruptions
- Security incidents
- Crew unavailability

### 3.2 Involuntary Rebooking Rules

| Rule | Detail |
|------|--------|
| **Fee** | No fee, regardless of fare family. |
| **Fare difference** | No fare difference charged, even if the new flight fare is higher. |
| **Cabin class** | Rebook in the same or higher cabin class. If only a lower cabin is available, passenger is entitled to a fare difference refund per EU261/2004. |
| **Airline obligation** | The airline must proactively offer rebooking options to all affected passengers. |
| **Interline rebooking** | If no same-airline flights are available within a reasonable timeframe, the airline may rebook on partner airlines or other carriers at no cost to the passenger. |
| **Ground transportation** | If the destination can be reached by ground transport within a reasonable time, the airline may offer bus/train transfer at no cost. |

### 3.3 Involuntary Rebooking Priority

When multiple passengers require involuntary rebooking and seat availability is limited, the following priority order applies:

1. Unaccompanied minors (UMNR)
2. Passengers with medical conditions or reduced mobility (MEDA, WCHR, WCHC)
3. Passengers with connecting flights at risk
4. Business Flex passengers
5. Business Saver passengers
6. Loyalty program Platinum members
7. Loyalty program Gold members
8. Flex passengers
9. Classic passengers
10. Light passengers
11. All other passengers (by check-in order)

### 3.4 Involuntary Rebooking Process

1. Identify all affected passengers from the disrupted flight's PNR list.
2. Run the automatic rebooking engine (AUTOREB) to assign passengers to the next available flights.
3. Review AUTOREB assignments for accuracy and passenger priority.
4. For passengers not automatically rebooked:
   a. Contact the passenger via SMS, email, and push notification.
   b. Offer at least **two alternative flight options** where possible.
   c. If no same-day flight is available, offer the **next available flight within 48 hours**.
5. If the passenger does not respond within **4 hours**, confirm the best available rebooking option automatically.
6. Issue meal/hotel vouchers as per care obligations (see Disruption Handling Guide).
7. Update the PNR with involuntary rebooking reason code and new flight details.
8. Send rebooking confirmation to the passenger.

## 4. Standby Rules

### 4.1 Voluntary Standby

- **Eligibility:** Flex, Business Saver, and Business Flex fare holders.
- **Fee:** Free.
- **Process:**
  1. Passenger requests standby for an earlier flight on the same day and route.
  2. Agent adds the passenger to the standby list in the departure control system (DCS).
  3. Passenger must be present at the gate **45 minutes before the standby flight's departure**.
  4. Gate agent clears standby passengers **15 minutes before departure**, based on:
     - Loyalty tier (highest first)
     - Fare family (Business Flex > Business Saver > Flex)
     - Check-in time (earliest first)
  5. If cleared, passenger boards the standby flight. Original flight booking is automatically cancelled.
  6. If not cleared, passenger returns to their original flight.
- **Baggage:** If standby is cleared at least 30 minutes before departure, checked baggage is transferred. Otherwise, passenger must travel with carry-on only and collect checked bags from the original flight's arrival.

### 4.2 Involuntary Standby

- In disruption situations, passengers may be placed on standby for the next available flight.
- Involuntary standby passengers receive **priority over voluntary standby** passengers.
- No fee applies.
- The airline must provide regular updates (every 30 minutes) on standby status.

## 5. Rebooking Windows

### 5.1 Same-Day Rebooking

- Available for all fare families except Light.
- Passenger may rebook to any flight **on the same calendar day** (based on departure city local time).
- Same-day rebooking fee: EUR 25 flat rate for Classic fares; Free for Flex and Business fares.
- Subject to seat availability.

### 5.2 Next Available Flight

- When the desired flight date is not the same day, the passenger may rebook to the **next available flight** on the same route.
- Standard rebooking fees and fare differences apply per fare family.
- "Next available" means the earliest flight with available seats in the same fare family or higher.

### 5.3 Rebooking Within 14 Days

- Classic fare passengers may rebook to any flight **within 14 calendar days** of the original departure date.
- Flex and Business fare passengers may rebook to any flight **within 12 months** of the original ticket issue date.
- Rebooking beyond these windows is treated as a cancellation and new purchase.

### 5.4 Open-Jaw Rebooking

- Passengers may rebook to an open-jaw itinerary (arriving at a different airport than the return departure) subject to:
  - Availability in the same fare family
  - Payment of any fare difference
  - Standard rebooking fees
  - The open-jaw routing must be within the same geographical region

## 6. Special Rebooking Scenarios

### 6.1 Codeshare Flights

- Rebooking to/from codeshare flights requires the marketing carrier's fare rules to be applied.
- If the original ticket was on a codeshare flight, rebooking to the operating carrier's own flight may require revalidation.
- Contact the interline desk (extension 3100) for complex codeshare rebookings.

### 6.2 Connecting Itineraries

- When rebooking a segment of a multi-segment itinerary, ensure the minimum connection time (MCT) is met at the connecting airport.
- If rebooking causes a misconnection, the agent must rebook the entire onward journey.
- Protecting the passenger on the next available connection takes priority over maintaining the original routing.

### 6.3 Group Rebookings

- Group rebookings (10+ passengers) must be processed through the Group Desk.
- Group rebooking deadline: **7 days before departure**.
- Partial group rebookings (splitting passengers from the group) require group PNR modification.
- Contact: groups@airline.com or extension 4500.

### 6.4 Award Ticket Rebooking

- Award tickets (booked with loyalty points/miles) follow the same rebooking rules as their equivalent fare family.
- Rebooking fees for award tickets are charged in **miles**, not EUR:
  - Classic equivalent: 5,000 miles
  - Flex equivalent: Free
- If no award availability exists on the new flight, the passenger may pay the cash fare difference to rebook on a revenue ticket.

## 7. Rebooking Refusal & Escalation

### 7.1 When to Refuse Rebooking

- Light fare passengers requesting voluntary rebooking.
- Any fare family if the request is made **less than 3 hours** before departure (for voluntary changes).
- Passengers on a security watchlist or with travel document issues.
- Tickets that have already been used (fully flown).
- Expired tickets (beyond the rebooking window).

### 7.2 Escalation Path

If a rebooking request falls outside standard policy:
1. **Level 1 — Supervisor:** Can approve one-time fee waivers up to EUR 100.
2. **Level 2 — Duty Manager:** Can approve interline rebooking and fare family upgrades.
3. **Level 3 — Customer Relations Manager:** Can approve full policy exceptions for VIP passengers or high-value customers.

---

## 8. Documentation & Compliance

- All rebookings must be documented in the PNR with:
  - Rebooking reason (voluntary/involuntary + specific reason code)
  - Agent ID and timestamp
  - Fees charged (or fee waiver authorization)
  - Original and new flight details
- Involuntary rebookings must reference the IROP event code from the Operations Control Centre.
- All rebooking communications to passengers must include their rights under EU261/2004 when applicable.
